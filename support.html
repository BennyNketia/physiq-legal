Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iOS Settings app → tap your Apple ID at the top → Subscriptions → Physiq → Cancel Subscription. Cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period.

How do I delete my account?

In the Physiq app, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your scans, measurements, meals, water logs, goals, photos, and profile within 30 days. This action cannot be undone.

How accurate are the body fat estimates?

Physiq's photo-based estimates have an error margin similar to handheld bioelectrical impedance scales. They're most useful as a trend over time rather than a single absolute measurement. For clinical accuracy, consult a DEXA scan.

Why does my scan look off?

Scan quality depends on lighting, pose, distance from the camera, and clothing. Use even lighting, stand 6–8 feet from your phone, wear form-fitting clothes, and follow the on-screen positioning guide. Inconsistent conditions between scans are the #1 cause of unrealistic week-to-week changes.

How accurate are meal calorie estimates?

Meal nutrition is estimated by AI from your photo, so it's an approximation — portion size, sauces, oils, and hidden ingredients can throw it off. For best results, photograph the whole plate from above in good lighting. You can tap any item to edit its amount or nutrition before or after logging. Estimates aren't suitable for medical dietary needs and don't identify allergens.

Why can I only scan a few meals a day?

Meal scans have a daily limit to keep the service fast and affordable. You can still log meals manually or edit existing entries once you've reached the limit. The count resets daily.

What happens to my meal photos?

Meal photos are stored privately and only you can see them. The full-size photo is deleted automatically after 7 days; a small thumbnail stays with the meal in your log until you delete the meal or your account.

How do I connect or disconnect Apple Health?

Physiq asks to connect Apple Health from the Nutrition tab. To change access later, open iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Physiq, and turn categories on or off. Physiq only reads steps, active energy, and body weight; it never writes to Apple Health, and this data stays on your iPhone.

My steps or calories burned aren't showing.

Check that Physiq has read access in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Physiq. Data from an Apple Watch or other devices may take a few minutes to sync to the Health app before Physiq can show it.

Can I request a refund?

All subscriptions are handled by Apple. To request a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID. Physiq does not process refunds directly.

How do I export my data?

Email us at stimtroop@gmail.com from the address on your Physiq account, and we'll send you a complete export within 14 days.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

Does Physiq work on Android?

Physiq is currently iOS-only. An Android version is on our roadmap — email us if you'd like to be notified at launch.
